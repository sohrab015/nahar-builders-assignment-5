# Content Research Notes: Nahar Builders

All facts on the website were taken from the official site, https://www.naharbuilders.com/ (read in November 2026). Nothing was invented. Where the source did not publish something (founding year, statistics, testimonials, social media accounts), the redesign leaves it out.

## 1. What does Nahar Builders do?
A Chattogram-based civil construction and real estate development company. It builds residential towers, commercial complexes and a healthcare tower, offers interior finishing, renovation, project supervision and CDA approval drawings, imports building materials, and owns a heavy machinery fleet. It also works as a sub-contractor on national infrastructure (Dhaka Metro Rail, Airport 3rd Terminal).

## 2. What should visitors understand?
- Who the company is and who leads it (Engr. Md. Jashim Uddin, Chairman & Managing Director, B.Sc. in Civil (CET), FIEB)
- Mission and vision
- The range of services, from drawings and approvals through construction and finishing
- Proof of capability: completed towers, the 19-storied hospital, owned machinery, registrations

## 3. Projects highlighted
All 12 projects published on /projects are included: 7 completed residential/hotel buildings, Patiya Nahar Park & Resort, the Dhaka Metro Rail & Airport 3rd Terminal sub-contract work, and 3 ongoing projects (Lohagara Trade Centre, Nahar Specialized Hospital, Nahar School Market). The hospital gets extra engineering detail from /hospital. Storey counts are used to draw the hero skyline.

## 5. UX improvements over the existing site
